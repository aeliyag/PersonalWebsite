Second summer at Akool

A few weeks ago I wrapped up my second summer at Akool. I worked closely with the product and engineering teams under Alicia Tseng. More than anything, I walked away with a few lessons that changed how I think about building products.

1. The user really does have the best feedback

Sometimes you just need to stop guessing and work directly with users.

I created Akool’s Artist in Residence program, hosting seven events and recruiting three creators to come into the office, experiment with the tools, and share feedback on their workflows. I worked with creators across AI short dramas, AI realism, and AI marketing, helping them find new ways to bring AI into their process.

One of the most rewarding parts was watching that feedback turn into both product improvements and creator growth.

2. AI is changing how we build

I independently owned the end-to-end implementation of several features for Akool’s Agentic Canvas, including AI style generation, prompt refinement, canvas navigation, and node cleanup.

Using LLM agents throughout development made me rethink how quickly an idea can move from product concept to something that actually ships.

3. Sometimes inspiration comes from your competitors

I did competitive research across AI swapping, agentic workflows, and new-user onboarding, and led four team-wide product strategy reviews.

The point was not just to see what competitors were doing. It was to figure out what we could learn from them, and where we could build something better.

4. Conversion is everything

A great product only matters if users actually make it through the funnel.

I focused heavily on the new-user experience: onboarding tutorials, and a React and TypeScript video editor I built to make producing customized tutorials faster. I also looked at how onboarding surveys and other product touchpoints could help us understand and convert new users.

I am incredibly grateful to the Akool team for another summer of learning, building, and getting closer to the users who make the product better. Thank you to everyone who made this summer such a meaningful experience.

What’s next
