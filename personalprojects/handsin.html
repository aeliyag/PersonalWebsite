Hands In

I co-founded Hands In and stepped back in fall 2025. The company has since raised $100K+.

Overview

AI-powered ASL learning platform that uses computer vision for real-time gesture recognition. I designed and launched the first course, completed by 50+ learners.

Site: handsinlearning.co

Role: co‑founder, CEO

Stack: PyTorch, MediaPipe, Vercel, Render, React.js, TensorFlow.js

Coding tools: Claude, Roo Code, ChatGPT

Languages: JS/HTML/CSS, Python

While involved, focus included product–market exploration, gathering user feedback, improving hand‑recognition, and infra (e.g. cold starts / Azure).
